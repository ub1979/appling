Keep the tide clean.

We clean beaches, restore seagrass and teach the next generation to look after the sea. Every pound goes to the coast.

Stop 1 · Fernhaven

Where it started.

Twelve volunteers, one beach, 400 kg of rubbish on the first morning. We still clean here every month.

Stop 2 · Gull Point

Nets off the rocks.

Divers lift lost fishing nets that trap seals and birds — over 30 tonnes since 2019.

Stop 3 · Seagrass Bay

Planting a meadow.

Seagrass stores carbon and shelters fish. We've replanted eleven hectares, seed by seed.

Stop 4 · Low Harbour

Teaching the next crew.

Every week a school class spends a day on the shore with our rangers.
