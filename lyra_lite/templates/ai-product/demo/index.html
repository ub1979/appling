How accurate are the answers?

No. Your content is used only to answer your team's questions.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first source in under ten minutes and are asking questions the same afternoon.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month; you can export your settings first.
