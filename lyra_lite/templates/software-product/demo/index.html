Questions

Good to know.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their banks and accounting system in an afternoon and run their first close with Ledgerline the same month.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No. Ledgerline sits on top of Xero, QuickBooks or NetSuite and posts the results back, so your ledger stays the source of truth.

Is our data safe?

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted in the EU or US, and covered by our SOC 2 Type II report.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the month; yearly plans at the end of the year. Your data exports in one click.
