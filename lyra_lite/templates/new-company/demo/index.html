01What we do

Four jobs, done end to end.

Most teams hire us for one of these and keep us for the rest.

  • We rework a prototype so a factory can build it: fewer parts, sensible tolerances, a bill of materials that survives a price rise.

  • Board layout, power budgets and the firmware that tests every unit on the line before it is boxed.

  • Shortlists, factory visits and tooling reviews, with quotes you can compare line by line.

  • We stand on the line for the first run, fix what breaks and write down how to build it again.

02How we work

Three rules we do not bend.

I

One engineer owns your product.

The person on your first call is the person on the factory floor. No hand-offs, no account managers.

II

Fixed price per phase.

Every phase has a written scope and a price before it starts. If we are wrong about effort, that is our cost.

III

You keep every file.

CAD, schematics, firmware and supplier contacts are yours from day one, in your own accounts.
